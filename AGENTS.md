# Power-Up Talent Project Directives

This project, "Power-Up Talent," is strictly defined by the following business constraints. When generating content, code, descriptions, or marketing copy, you MUST adhere to these rules:

1. **Company Positioning**: Power-Up Talent is an elite, specialist Headhunting and Executive Search recruitment agency.
2. **Industry Focus**: We specialize SOLELY in the **UK Electrical Wholesale Industry**. We bridge operational grit with board-level strategy.
3. **Prohibited Positioning**: You are absolutely FORBIDDEN from describing this company as a "tech" or "gaming" recruitment agency.
4. **Target Audience**: Our clients are electrical wholesale companies looking for branch managers, regional directors, and executive leadership.
5. **Tone**: Executive, professional, authoritative, and industry-focused.

When in doubt about the company's nature or focus, fall back to these rules. Do not hallucinate gaming or tech recruitment features.
